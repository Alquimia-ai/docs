**Workspace** shows the registry the active workspace is. Open it from **Settings**, under the sidebar's **Workspace** section; it is the first tab.

{/* screenshot: workspace-page */}

| Card | What it shows |
|------|---------------|
| **Identity** | Workspace ID, OCI address (`namespace/name:version`), owner, organization, dates. |
| **Access** | Visibility, status, and whether network egress is restricted. |
| **Distribution** | The repository it was pulled from, where it is published, and the last pull and push. |
| **Agents on hold** | Agents that refuse inference until released. |

## Editing

**Edit** changes the name, description, visibility and status. Org admins can edit workspaces of their organization; platform admins any workspace.

| Visibility | Who can open it |
|------------|-----------------|
| Private | Its owner and members of its organization. |
| Protected | Reserved by the platform; Studio treats it like private. |
| Public | Anyone in an organization that can see it. |

A **disabled** workspace is hidden from the switcher. The default workspace is shared with everyone on the deployment, keeps its name and cannot be disabled or deleted.

**Versions** opens the workspace's published versions. See [Versions](/platform/versions).

## Deleting

Deleting a workspace removes its agents, registrations and secret values. Published versions stay in the OCI registry and can be pulled again from Discover.
