With the stack running, open Studio in your browser:

**http://localhost:3001**

## Logging in

Studio supports **Keycloak** (enterprise SSO) or **Lite** login (local credentials), depending on how your stack is configured.

{/* screenshot: keycloak-login-screen */}

Enter the credentials configured in your `.env` file (the defaults are in `.env.example`). After logging in, you'll land on the Studio dashboard.

## If your account is pending approval

Signing in with an enterprise identity provider (Google through Keycloak, for example) creates
your Studio account the first time you use it — but it does not give it a role. When that
happens you land on a **pending approval** screen instead of the dashboard:

> Your account is pending approval. You signed in successfully, but no role has been assigned
> to your account yet.

Nothing is wrong with your credentials. Ask your platform administrator to assign you a role,
then sign out and back in to pick it up. See [Roles & Permissions](/platform/reference/roles-and-permissions)
for what each role can do.

<Note>
  If you can reach the dashboard but actions are greyed out, you have a role — just a
  read-only one. That is the `viewer` role, not a pending account.
</Note>

## If your session expires

Studio renews your access token in the background. If it cannot — because the identity
provider ended your session, or an administrator revoked it — you are taken to an
**authentication error** page saying your session expired, with a button to sign in again.
Nothing you were working on is lost that had already been saved.

## The onboarding tour

On your first visit, Studio starts an onboarding tour automatically. It highlights the key areas of the app:

1. **Dashboard** — your agent feed
2. **Tools** — the tools your agents may use, and what each may do
3. **Elements** — Models, Knowledge, Tools, Shields and Voice, each on its own page
4. **Discover** — the community agent catalog

{/* screenshot: onboarding-tour-start */}

You can dismiss the tour at any time and restart it from the sidebar if needed.

## Before creating your first agent

<Warning>
  The **Create Agent** button in the sidebar is **disabled until at least one model is configured**. Go to **Models** in the sidebar and add a model first.
</Warning>

If the models list is empty, [add a model under Models](/platform/settings/models) before creating an agent.

## The sidebar

The sidebar is your main navigation:

- **Dashboard** — view and manage your agents
- **Create Agent** — open the agent creation wizard (disabled without a model)
- **Discover** — browse and install community agents
- **Elements** — models, knowledge, tools, shields and voice: what agents are built from
- **Workspace** — secrets, activity, and settings (workspace, network egress, webhooks, TrustLens)
- **Workspace switcher** (top of sidebar) — switch between workspaces

**My credentials**, your own values for per-user secrets, is the second tab of **Secrets**.
